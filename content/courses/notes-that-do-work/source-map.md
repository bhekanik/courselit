## Lesson trace

| Lesson | Source spine | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Find where useful thinking disappears | `Knowledge Work Should Accrete.md`, `The art of note making.md` | A work-leak map is a diagnostic exercise, not a productivity measurement. |
| 02 Turn a capture into a source note | `The art of note making.md`, `0. Inbox/Readwise/Articles/Types of notes.md` | Rewriting in your own words helps expose understanding; no memory or retention claim. |
| 03 Give each note one job | `0. Inbox/Readwise/Articles/Types of notes.md`, `Evergreen Notes.md` | The note jobs are a practical model, not a universal ontology. |
| 04 Write one idea so it can stand alone | `Evergreen Notes.md`, `Documenting Domain-Specific Knowledge.md` | Atomicity means one usable claim or decision, not an arbitrary word count. |
| 05 Use tags, properties and links for different jobs | `Obsidian Organisation Philosophy.md` | The three-way distinction is BK's method. Other tools may express it differently. |
| 06 Search before creating | `Read Existing Before Creating.md`, `Knowledge Bank as Agent Working Memory.md` | Search-before-create protects against duplicate or stale notes; it does not guarantee completeness. |
| 07 Write to discover what you think | `Thinking on Paper.md`, `Writing is thinking.md`, `Write Notes for Yourself by Default, Disregarding Audience.md` | Discovery and presentation are different passes. No claim that writing is the only way to think. |
| 08 Build a synthesis note around a live question | `Notes Should Surprise You.md`, `Create Speculative Outlines While You Write.md` | Synthesis is organised around a live question and evidence, not around collecting every related note. |
| 09 Move from notes to an outline and a decision | `Executable Strategy for Writing.md`, `Evergreen Notes Permit Smooth Incremental Progress in Writing.md` | Existing notes reduce rework only when they are relevant and checked. |
| 10 Close the loop so the next job starts ahead | `Knowledge Work Should Accrete.md`, `Knowledge Bank as Agent Working Memory.md`, `Documenting Domain-Specific Knowledge.md` | Reuse depends on a real update trigger and owner. Nothing improves automatically. |

## Explicit exclusions

- No percentage or time-saving promise.
- No claim that connected notes automatically create insight.
- No claim that writing notes in your own words guarantees recall.
- No claim that folders, tags, properties or links have one correct arrangement.
- No claim that Obsidian is safer, more private or more durable than another tool without a reviewed source and organisational context.
- No shipping of private vault content, client information, employer information or unpublished project details.
