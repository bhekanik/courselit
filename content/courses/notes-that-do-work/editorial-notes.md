# Notes that do work: editorial notes

## Voice

Write like a practitioner showing a colleague what to do at the desk beside them. Keep the terms `capture`, `source note`, `concept note`, `synthesis note` and `working brief` stable. Do not turn each paragraph into a slogan.

Use examples before abstractions. Prefer the actual object:

- `the exception brief` rather than `the deliverable`;
- `the matter handover` rather than `knowledge transfer`;
- `the source page and paragraph` rather than `provenance` when the formal term is not needed;
- `what changes next time` rather than `continuous improvement`.

## Pedagogy

The course loop is:

1. Pick one real, bounded question.
2. Expose the learner's current model before explaining.
3. Show the decision in a worked case.
4. Guide the same decision on the learner's work.
5. Stop and record evidence.
6. Ask for a teach-back with the lesson closed.
7. Change the case.
8. Check the artefact.
9. Offer three hints without doing the work for the learner.

Each lesson owns one decision. If a section needs two decisions, give them separate lessons.

## Worked example thread

Use four professional cases across the course rather than inventing a different scenario in every paragraph:

- Accountant: month-end exception notes become a review brief.
- Lawyer: matter notes and authorities become a handover or advice outline. The lawyer checks sources and professional duties.
- Operations lead: incident and meeting records become a decision note with owners.
- Analyst: reading and observations become a recommendation with uncertainty visible.

The examples illustrate the method. They are not legal, accounting or records-management advice.

## Capstone distinction

The ten lesson artefacts are working records. The capstone's four final files are the evidence bundle from one fresh run. Do not claim that every setup note was created during the capstone run.

Every diagram must answer a question the learner is deciding at that point. A decorative screenshot of a graph view is not a teaching diagram.

## Copy guard

Run Humanizer, BK voice review and Layer A mark removal. Keep deliberate asymmetry. A short line is allowed when it earns the pause. Do not build every section from a balanced three-item list. No em dash, no faux quotation marks around ordinary terms and no generic final paragraph about transformation.
