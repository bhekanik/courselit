# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Working professionals in operations, product, research, analysis, marketing, law, finance and software. They have tried an AI chat tool but still use it one question at a time. They arrive with one recurring, low-risk job, limited time, and accountability for what leaves their hands. Coding is not required.

## Product Purpose

AI Work School teaches people to turn a real job into an inspectable AI-assisted workflow. The free course helps each learner state the job, supply durable context, choose the right level of automation, connect only approved sources, set checks that can fail, record decisions and hand the work over. Success is a completed real run whose evidence and failure path another person can inspect.

## Brand Personality

Assured, practical, human. The voice is that of an experienced colleague at the same table: specific about the work, calm about limits, and generous with explanation.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI gradients, purple glow, robot or chatbot art, and synthetic intelligence motifs.
- Giant empty heroes, endless identical card grids, marquees, glassmorphism and motion without a job.
- Editorial-magazine styling used as a shortcut for seriousness.
- Tiny uppercase eyebrows, generic numbered section scaffolding, fake metrics, testimonials and unsupported time-saving claims.
- Tool rankings or brand loyalty presented as durable professional judgement.

## Design Principles

1. **Put the work in the room.** Show documents, sources, checks and decisions before naming AI concepts.
2. **Make the checks visible.** A learner should see where evidence enters, where work can stop, and where a person decides.
3. **Use the lowest sufficient mechanism.** Explain chat, reusable skills, workflows and governed connections through the job they solve, not their novelty.
4. **Earn every visual.** Imagery depicts real professional work. Diagrams teach an exact relationship or sequence. Decoration does neither and is removed.
5. **Keep the next action close.** The free-course action appears in the header and before the first mobile viewport ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark modes. Body text and controls meet 4.5:1 contrast; large text meets 3:1; focus indicators and meaningful boundaries meet 3:1 non-text contrast. Colour never carries meaning alone. Diagrams have visible labels, a defined reading order and equivalent alt text. Motion is bounded, never required to understand content, and removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Copy uses British English and assumes neither technical vocabulary nor coding experience.
